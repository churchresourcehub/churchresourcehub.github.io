Printing this file

  • Print from Chrome. In the print dialog set Margins: None and turn ON "Background graphics," or the water panels and header bars will come out white.
  • Every sheet is 11 × 8.5 in landscape, so the whole job runs on one paper size. Sheets 1 and 2 are the tri-fold, printed two-sided and flipped on the short edge. Sheet 3 is the short card, two-up, cut once down the middle into 5.5 × 8.5 in cards.
  • Fold: cover panel faces out, the narrow panel tucks inside. Panel widths are 3.625 / 3.6875 / 3.6875 in for the roll fold.
  • For a commercial print run, hand this file plus the copy to the printer or rebuild in InDesign at these measurements with 0.125 in bleed.
  • The QR code is live and points to the ShelbyNext commitment form already linked on the website.
Why We Are Asking2027

Moses told Israel they would inherit a land full of good things even though they had done nothing to produce it. Then he warned them about the one thing that could ruin it: forgetting where it came from.

Nearly everything we love about this church was paid for and prayed over by people we never met. We are the recipients of the grace and generosity of those who came before us. We know what it costs to keep this well we call the church open and operating at a high level. It takes a congregation full of people who are willing to sacrifice their time, offer their leadership, share their gifts, practice generosity, and pray relentlessly. I am grateful to the people who did this before we were ever around. And the water continues to flow because of your faithfulness to God and this church.

This card represents your promise to join in this work. It represents your commitment to be someone who offers the ministry, in addition to receiving it. Some of us are reopening a well that has been closed for decades, and some of us are picking up a shovel here for the first time. No matter where you are on your journey, you are invited to experience the living water of God, here at SFUMC.

Grace and Peace,
Woods
